What we check

10 threat checks. One scan.

Each scan runs a full-spectrum check across your domain's infrastructure, security posture, and brand exposure.

🔒
SSL Certificate Health
Checks if your HTTPS certificate is valid, who issued it, and alerts you before it expires.
📧
Email Security (DNS)
Verifies SPF, DMARC & DKIM so attackers can't send fake emails pretending to be your business.
🌐
Web Surface Exposure
Detects accidentally exposed files like .env, .git configs, and admin backup files.
🛡️
Brand Impersonation
Alerts you if look-alike domains are registered to trick your customers into sending payments elsewhere.
📍
Server Geolocation
Shows where your server is physically hosted and flags proxy, VPN, or datacenter IPs.
💀
CVE Vulnerability Check
Matches your running services against the global NIST database of known exploits.
🧱
HTTP Security Headers
Checks 6 critical browser security headers that protect your visitors from attacks.
🏗️
CMS Detection
Identifies if your site runs WordPress, Joomla, or Drupal and flags known vulnerabilities.
🔑
Default Credentials
Tests whether your admin panels are using common default passwords that attackers already know.
🌍
Subdomain Enumeration
Discovers hidden subdomains like dev, staging, and api that may be running unprotected.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

You don't need to install anything or know what an IP address is.

1
Enter your business domain
Type your website address like yourbusiness.com into the scan box. No account or login required.
2
We run 10 automated security checks
Our engine scans your SSL, DNS records, open ports, exposed files, brand lookalikes, known CVEs, server location, and more — all in under 60 seconds.

A score from 0–100 shows how safe your business is. If your score is below 80, you receive a prioritized Roadmap to Recovery — a ranked list of exactly what to fix first.

Understanding your score

What your score means

Calculated using the formula Σ(Vulnerability × Severity) / Total Checks — the same standard used by enterprise security teams.

80–100
Highly Resilient
Your digital storefront is well-protected. Keep scanning regularly to stay ahead of new threats.
50–79
Action Required
Multiple vulnerabilities found. Use the Roadmap to Recovery to fix the highest-risk issues first.
0–49
Critical Danger
Your infrastructure is severely exposed. Immediate action needed to prevent data loss or downtime.
